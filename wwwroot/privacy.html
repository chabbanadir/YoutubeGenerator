Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Summoner's Spells collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect and manage your accounts on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • OAuth Authentication Tokens: We collect and store encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens to publish authorized video content on your connected YouTube and TikTok accounts.
  • Channel / Profile Information: Basic public profile identifiers (account ID, handle) necessary to verify publication targets.
  • Video Metadata: Titles, descriptions, hashtags, and rendering logs generated for scheduled publications.

2. How Information Is Used

We use authentication credentials solely to execute user-configured video uploads and status tracking via official APIs. We never sell, rent, or share personal or account data with any third parties.

3. Third-Party API Services

Summoner's Spells interfaces with third-party developer APIs:

  • TikTok API: Operates in strict accordance with the TikTok Privacy Policy and Developer Terms.
  • YouTube API Services: Operates in compliance with the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request complete deletion of stored credentials and tokens at any time by contacting us or revoking access via your account security dashboard. Revoked tokens are immediately purged from our database.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data privacy inquiries, please reach out to privacy@summonerspells.app.
