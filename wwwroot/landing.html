How Summoner's Spells Works

Simple 3-step pipeline built to automate short-form distribution without manual editing overhead.

1

Authorize Your Channels

Creators connect their verified TikTok or YouTube accounts using official OAuth 2.0 authorization flows. Tokens are securely encrypted and can be revoked at any time.

  • TikTok Login Kit compliant
  • Scopes: video.publish & video.upload
2

Automated Highlight Processing

Our engine ingests recorded gameplay, detects peak action moments, and renders high-definition vertical 1080x1920 clips with dynamic audio normalization and safe-zone overlay alignment.

  • 60 FPS H.264 / AAC Encoding
  • Optimized for mobile full-screen view
3

Direct Publishing & Scheduling

Clips are automatically queued and published to your authorized TikTok creator profile via the TikTok Content Posting API with compliant titles, hashtags, and privacy settings.

  • Rate-limit safe scheduling cadence
  • Full publish confirmation telemetry
